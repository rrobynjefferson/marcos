Fitz Roy Trekking in Argentina's Patagonia is a unique and must-visit destination for adventure travelers. * Nature: The area is home to diverse wildlife, including guanacos, condors, and pumas, and offers breathtaking views of mountains, glaciers, and rivers.

The visual and sensory highlights of Fitz Roy Trekking Argentina Patagonia include:

* The stunning mountain peaks of Fitz Roy and Cerro Torre

### From Gateway City to Location (Last Mile)
From El Calafate, you can take a bus or drive to El Chaltén, the starting point of the Fitz Roy trek. The journey takes around 3-4 hours by bus or 2-3 hours by car. Some recommended bus companies include:

* Taqsa

The bus ticket price range is:

## 📋 Visa, Permits & Regulations
Citizens of the US, Canada, and EU countries do not need a visa to enter Argentina. However, you will need to obtain a reciprocity fee, which costs $160 and is valid for 10 years.

To enter Los Glaciares National Park, you will need to obtain a park entry permit, which costs $20 per person. You can purchase the permit at the park entrance or online.

It is recommended to hire a local guide for the trek, as they can provide valuable insights and help you navigate the terrain. Guide fees range from $100 to $300 per day.
