## 🌄 Why Overland Track Tasmania Australia Complete Travel Guide?
The Overland Track in Tasmania, Australia, is a unique and must-visit destination for adventure travelers. This 65 km track offers breathtaking views of the Tasmanian Wilderness World Heritage Area, including Cradle Mountain, Barn Bluff, and Mount Ossa. The track takes you through a variety of landscapes, including rainforests, moorlands, and alpine meadows.

* **Unique experiences:** Trekking through the Tasmanian Wilderness, spotting native wildlife such as quolls and platypus, and taking in the stunning views from the summit of Mount Ossa.
* **Visual and sensory highlights:** The track offers a range of visual and sensory experiences, including the sound of rushing water, the smell of eucalyptus, and the sight of towering mountain peaks.
* **Best reasons to visit:** The Overland Track offers a range of reasons to visit, including spiritual growth, physical challenge, connection with nature, and cultural experiences.

### From Gateway City to Location (Last Mile)
From Hobart, you can take a bus or drive to the start of the Overland Track at Cradle Mountain National Park.

* **Nearest local city with an airport/train station:** Launceston (LST) and Devonport (DPO) are the closest cities with airports, but you can also take a bus from Hobart.
* **Ground transport:** TassieLink and McDermott's Coaches offer bus services from Hobart to Cradle Mountain National Park. You can also take a taxi or shuttle service.
* **REALISTIC travel duration:** The bus journey from Hobart to Cradle Mountain National Park takes around 4-5 hours.
* **Important transit points and checkpoints:** You'll need to stop at the Cradle Mountain National Park visitor center to register and pay for your park entry fee.
* **Ferry/boat crossing:** Not required for the Overland Track.

### Overland/Backpacker Route Options
If you're on a budget or prefer to travel overland, you can take a bus from Melbourne or Sydney to Hobart and then connect to Cradle Mountain National Park.

* **Alternative land routes:** You can take the Spirit of Tasmania ferry from Melbourne to Devonport and then take a bus to Cradle Mountain National Park.
* **Border crossings:** Not required for the Overland Track.

## 🗓️ Best Time to Visit
The best time to visit the Overland Track is from December to March, when the weather is warm and sunny.

* **Peak season:** December to March, with the busiest period being January and February.
* **Off-season:** April to November, with the quietest period being June and July.
* **Weather conditions per season:**
	+ Summer (December to February): Warm and sunny, with average temperatures ranging from 17°C to 23°C.
	+ Autumn (March to May): Mild and cool, with average temperatures ranging from 10°C to 18°C.
	+ Winter (June to August): Cold and wet, with average temperatures ranging from 2°C to 10°C.
	+ Spring (September to November): Cool and wet, with average temperatures ranging from 5°C to 15°C.
* **Local festivals/events:** The Cradle Mountain Film Festival is held in February, and the Tasmanian Wilderness World Heritage Area offers guided walks and tours throughout the year.
* **WARNING:** Avoid visiting during the winter months (June to August) due to the cold and wet weather.
