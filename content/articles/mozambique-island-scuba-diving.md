## 🌄 Why Mozambique Island Scuba Diving Complete Travel Guide? (Key Attractions)
Mozambique Island, located off the coast of northern Mozambique, is a hidden gem for scuba diving enthusiasts. The island offers a unique blend of coral reefs, shipwrecks, and an incredible array of marine life, making it a must-visit destination for any serious diver. The crystal-clear waters of the Indian Ocean provide visibility of up to 40 meters, allowing divers to fully appreciate the vibrant colors and diverse species of the underwater world.

Some of the key attractions of Mozambique Island scuba diving include:
* The opportunity to dive on coral reefs, including the famous San Sebastian Reef
* Exploring shipwrecks, such as the 19th-century Portuguese ship, the "Santo Antonio"
* Swimming with an incredible array of marine life, including manta rays, whale sharks, and sea turtles
* Discovering the island's rich history and culture, including the UNESCO World Heritage Site of the old town

The best reasons to visit Mozambique Island for scuba diving include:
* Spiritual: the opportunity to connect with nature and experience the beauty of the underwater world
* Physical challenge: the chance to push your limits and try new diving experiences
* Nature: the incredible array of marine life and coral reefs
* Culture: the opportunity to learn about the island's rich history and culture

### From Gateway City to Location (Last Mile)
From Nampula, you can take a bus or taxi to the town of Nacala, which is located about 30 km from the port city of Mozambique Island. The bus journey takes about 4-5 hours and costs around $10-20 USD. The taxi journey takes about 3-4 hours and costs around $50-100 USD.

From Nacala, you can take a ferry to Mozambique Island. The ferry journey takes about 1-2 hours and costs around $10-20 USD. The ferry operates daily, but the schedule may vary depending on the season.

Some of the ground transport options from Nampula to Nacala include:
* Bus: Chapas (minibus) or Intercape bus
* Taxi: Nacala Taxi or Mozambique Island Taxi
* Private transfer: can be arranged through your hotel or a private transfer company

Important transit points and checkpoints include:
* Nampula Airport
* Nacala Bus Station

## 🗓️ Best Time to Visit
The best time to visit Mozambique Island for scuba diving is during the dry season, which runs from April to September. During this time, the weather is calm and the visibility is excellent, making it ideal for diving.

The peak season for tourism is from June to August, when the weather is cooler and the island is less crowded. The off-season is from October to March, when the weather is hotter and more humid.

Some of the local festivals and events worth attending include:
* Mozambique Island Festival: a cultural festival that takes place in August
* Nacala Festival: a music festival that takes place in September

WARNING: The bad season to avoid is from October to March, when the weather is hotter and more humid, and the island is prone to cyclones.

## 📋 Visa, Permits & Regulations
Visa requirements for Mozambique include:
* A valid passport with at least six months' validity
* A visa, which can be obtained on arrival or in advance through the Mozambican embassy or consulate

Park entry permits are required for some of the island's historic sites and natural reserves. The permits can be obtained through the Mozambican National Parks and Reserves Authority.

A local guide is not mandatory, but it is recommended to hire a guide for scuba diving and cultural tours.

Environmental regulations include:
* No touching or standing on coral reefs
* No feeding or touching marine life
* No littering or polluting the environment
