## 🗓️ Best Time to Visit
The best time to visit the Amazon Jungle in Brazil depends on the season and your preferences.

- **Peak season (June to November)**: This is the dry season, and the weather is generally sunny and dry.
- **Off-season (December to May)**: This is the wet season, and the weather can be rainy and humid.

Weather conditions per season:

- **Dry season**: Sunny and dry, with average temperatures ranging from 20-30°C (68-86°F).
- **Wet season**: Rainy and humid, with average temperatures ranging from 20-30°C (68-86°F).

Local festivals and events:

- **Festa de São João**: A festival held in June, which features music, dance, and food.
