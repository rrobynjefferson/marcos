## 🗓️ Best Time to Visit
The best time to trek the Haute Route Chamonix Zermatt is from June to September, when the weather is warm and sunny, and the snow has melted from the higher peaks.
- **Peak Season**: July and August are the peak months, with warm weather and long days. However, this is also the busiest time, with many trekkers and tourists on the trail.
- **Off-Season**: June and September are considered the off-season, with cooler weather and fewer tourists. However, this can be a great time to trek, with more peaceful trails and lower prices.
