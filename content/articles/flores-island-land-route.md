## 🌄 Why Flores Island Land Route Complete Travel Guide?
Flores Island is a hidden gem in Eastern Indonesia, offering a unique blend of natural beauty, rich culture, and exciting adventure activities. The island is home to numerous traditional villages, stunning waterfalls, and picturesque lakes, making it a must-visit destination for any traveler. The Flores Island Land Route is a scenic and challenging journey that takes you through the island's rugged terrain, offering breathtaking views of the surrounding landscape.

Some of the key attractions on Flores Island include:

* **Kelimutu National Park**: A UNESCO World Heritage Site and home to the famous three-colored lakes.
* **Riung 17 Islands Marine Park**: A stunning marine park with crystal-clear waters and an incredible array of marine life.
* **Wae Rebo Village**: A traditional Manggarai village with unique architecture and stunning views of the surrounding landscape.
* **Labuanbajo**: A charming town with a rich history and stunning views of the surrounding islands.

The island's unique culture, stunning natural beauty, and exciting adventure activities make it an ideal destination for travelers looking for a challenging and rewarding experience.

### Overland/Backpacker Route Options
For budget travelers, there are several overland route options available. One of the most popular options is to take the bus from **Bali** to **Lombok** and then from **Lombok** to **Sumbawa**. From **Sumbawa**, you can take a ferry to **Flores**.

Some recommended bus companies for this route include:

* **Damas**: Offers bus services from Bali to Lombok and Sumbawa.
* **Gunung Mas**: Offers bus services from Bali to Lombok and Sumbawa.

The estimated travel duration for this route is around 24-30 hours, depending on the road conditions and traffic.

## 🗓️ Best Time to Visit
The best time to visit Flores Island is during the **dry season (May-September)**, when the weather is dry and sunny. The **peak season** is during **June-August**, when the weather is at its best and the island is busiest.

The **off-season** is during **October-April**, when the weather is wet and rainy. However, this can be a good time to visit if you're looking for a more relaxed atmosphere and lower prices.

Some local festivals and events worth attending include:

* **Reba Festival**: A traditional festival held in **Ruteng** to celebrate the island's rich culture and heritage.
* **Labuanbajo Festival**: A festival held in **Labuanbajo** to celebrate the island's marine life and natural beauty.

## 🥾 Adventure Activities & Itinerary
Here's a sample 7-day itinerary for the Flores Island Land Route:

### Day 1 — Arrival in Labuanbajo
* Arrive in **Labuanbajo** and check into your hotel.
* Spend the day exploring the town and its surroundings.
* Visit the **Labuanbajo Market** to try some local food and buy some souvenirs.

### Day 2 — Labuanbajo to Ruteng
* Take a bus or taxi to **Ruteng** (around 4-5 hours).
* Visit the **Ruteng Pu'u Village** to see the traditional Manggarai houses.
* Explore the **Ruteng Market** to try some local food and buy some souvenirs.

### Day 3 — Ruteng to Wae Rebo
* Take a bus or taxi to **Wae Rebo** (around 2-3 hours).
* Hike to the **Wae Rebo Village** (around 3-4 hours) to see the traditional village and stunning views.
* Spend the night in **Wae Rebo**.

### Day 4 — Wae Rebo to Kelimutu National Park
* Hike back to the starting point (around 3-4 hours).
* Take a bus or taxi to **Kelimutu National Park** (around 2-3 hours).
* Explore the **Kelimutu National Park** and see the three-colored lakes.

### Day 5 — Kelimutu National Park
* Spend the day exploring the **Kelimutu National Park**.
* Hike to the **Kelimutu Crater** (around 2-3 hours) to see the stunning views.
* Visit the **Kelimutu Museum** to learn more about the park's history and geology.

### Day 6 — Kelimutu National Park to Riung 17 Islands Marine Park
* Take a bus or taxi to **Riung 17 Islands Marine Park** (around 2-3 hours).
* Explore the **Riung 17 Islands Marine Park** and see the stunning marine life.
* Spend the night in **Riung**.

### Day 7 — Riung 17 Islands Marine Park
* Spend the day exploring the **Riung 17 Islands Marine Park**.
* Go snorkeling or diving to see the stunning marine life.
* Return to **Labuanbajo** (around 2-3 hours) for your departure.

## 📋 Visa, Permits & Regulations
To enter Indonesia, you'll need a valid passport with at least six months' validity. You can obtain a **visa on arrival** at most international airports, including **Ngurah Rai International Airport** in Bali. The cost of a visa on arrival is around $35-$50 USD.

To visit Flores Island, you'll need to obtain a **permit** from the local authorities. The cost of a permit is around $10-$20 USD per person.

Some recommended regulations to follow include:

* **Respect local customs**: Dress modestly and respect local traditions.
* **Protect the environment**: Take your trash with you and avoid littering.
* **Follow safety guidelines**: Always follow safety guidelines and instructions from your guide or local authorities.

## ⚠️ Safety & Risks
Some safety risks to be aware of on Flores Island include:

* **Altitude sickness**: Be aware of the risks of altitude sickness when hiking to high-altitude areas.
* **Wildlife**: Be aware of the risks of wildlife encounters, such as snakes and spiders.
* **Weather**: Be prepared for unpredictable weather conditions, such as rain and storms.
* **Crime**: Be aware of the risks of crime, such as theft and pickpocketing.

Some recommended emergency contacts include:

* **Local hospital**: **Ruteng Hospital** (around $10-$20 USD per consultation).
* **Medical evac**: **Bali International Medical Centre** (around $100-$500 USD per evacuation).
