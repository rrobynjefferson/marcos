## 🌄 Why Mount Kenya Trekking Routes Complete Travel Guide?
Mount Kenya, the second-highest peak in Africa, offers a unique and challenging trekking experience. The mountain's diverse landscapes, ranging from tropical rainforests to alpine deserts, make it a must-visit destination for adventure seekers. The trekking routes provide breathtaking views of the surrounding countryside, including the stunning Lake Ellis and the picturesque Gathiuru Forest. The best reasons to visit Mount Kenya include its spiritual significance, physical challenge, and unparalleled natural beauty.

* Unique experiences:
	+ Watching the sunrise from the summit of Point Lenana
	+ Exploring the diverse flora and fauna of the mountain
	+ Visiting the traditional villages of the Kikuyu people
* Visual/sensory highlights:
	+ The stunning views of the surrounding countryside
	+ The vibrant colors of the alpine flowers
	+ The sound of the mountain streams and waterfalls
* Best reasons:
	+ Spiritual: The mountain is considered sacred by the local Kikuyu people
	+ Physical challenge: The trekking routes offer a challenging and rewarding experience
	+ Nature: The mountain's diverse landscapes and unique flora and fauna
	+ Culture: The opportunity to visit traditional villages and learn about the local culture

### From Gateway City to Location (Last Mile)
From Nairobi, you can take a bus or taxi to the town of Nanyuki, which is the nearest town to Mount Kenya. The journey takes around 3-4 hours by car or 4-5 hours by bus. You can also take a domestic flight from Nairobi to Nanyuki Airport (NYK) with airlines such as Safarilink or Air Kenya.

* Ground transport:
	+ Bus: Companies such as Easy Coach and Modern Coast Express operate daily services from Nairobi to Nanyuki
	+ Taxi: Available at the airport or in the city center
	+ Car rental: Companies such as Europcar and Hertz have offices in Nairobi
* REALISTIC travel duration:
	+ By car: 3-4 hours
	+ By bus: 4-5 hours
* Important transit points and checkpoints:
	+ The town of Thika
	+ The town of Karatina
* If ferry/boat crossing is needed: Not applicable

### Overland/Backpacker Route Options
For budget travelers, there are alternative land routes to get to Mount Kenya. You can take a bus from Nairobi to the town of Meru, and then transfer to a local matatu (minibus) to the town of Chogoria. From Chogoria, you can take a taxi or hike to the Mount Kenya National Park.

* Alternative land routes:
	+ Nairobi to Meru: Bus companies such as Easy Coach and Modern Coast Express operate daily services
	+ Meru to Chogoria: Local matatu (minibus) services are available
* Border crossings: Not applicable

## 🗓️ Best Time to Visit
The best time to visit Mount Kenya is during the dry season, from June to October. The weather is generally clear and sunny, with average temperatures ranging from 10°C to 20°C. The wet season, from November to May, is characterized by heavy rainfall and mist, making it more challenging to trek.

* Peak season: June to October
* Off-season: November to May
* Weather conditions:
	+ Dry season: Clear and sunny, with average temperatures ranging from 10°C to 20°C
	+ Wet season: Heavy rainfall and mist, with average temperatures ranging from 5°C to 15°C
* Local festivals/events:
	+ The Mount Kenya Marathon, held in June
	+ The Nanyuki Cultural Festival, held in August
* WARNING: Bad seasons to avoid:
	+ The wet season, from November to May, due to heavy rainfall and mist
	+ The cold season, from July to August, due to low temperatures
