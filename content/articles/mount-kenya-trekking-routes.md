## 🌄 Why Mount Kenya Trekking Routes Complete Travel Guide?
Mount Kenya, the second-highest peak in Africa, offers a unique and challenging trekking experience. The mountain's diverse landscapes, ranging from tropical rainforests to arctic-like conditions, make it an attractive destination for adventure seekers. The trekking routes on Mount Kenya provide breathtaking views of the surrounding landscape, including the glaciers, lakes, and valleys. The experience is not just about reaching the summit but also about immersing oneself in the local culture and witnessing the incredible biodiversity of the region.

The trekking routes on Mount Kenya are designed to cater to different skill levels, from beginner to experienced climbers. The most popular routes include the Sirimon Route, the Naro Moru Route, and the Burguret Route. Each route offers a unique perspective on the mountain and its surroundings, making it an exciting experience for trekkers.

The best reasons to trek Mount Kenya include the spiritual experience of standing on the summit, the physical challenge of conquering the mountain, and the opportunity to connect with nature and local culture. The trekking experience is also an excellent way to challenge oneself, push personal limits, and develop a sense of accomplishment.

### From Gateway City to Location (Last Mile)
From Nairobi, trekkers can take a bus or taxi to the town of Nanyuki, which is the closest town to Mount Kenya. The journey takes approximately 3-4 hours by car or 4-5 hours by bus.

* Nearest local city with an airport: Nanyuki Airport (NYK)
* Ground transport: buses operated by companies such as Easy Coach, Modern Coast, and Simba Coach
* REALISTIC travel duration: 3-4 hours by car, 4-5 hours by bus
* Important transit points and checkpoints: Nairobi, Nanyuki, and the Mount Kenya National Park gate

### Overland/Backpacker Route Options
For budget travelers, there are alternative land routes to reach Mount Kenya. One option is to take a bus from Nairobi to the town of Meru, and then transfer to a local matatu (minibus) to reach the town of Chogoria. From Chogoria, trekkers can take a taxi or walk to the Mount Kenya National Park gate.

* Alternative land routes: Nairobi - Meru - Chogoria - Mount Kenya National Park
* Border crossings: none
* Recommended bus companies: Easy Coach, Modern Coast, and Simba Coach

## 🗓️ Best Time to Visit
The best time to trek Mount Kenya is during the dry season, which runs from June to October. During this period, the weather is generally clear, and the trails are less slippery.

* Peak season: June to October
* Off-season: November to May
* Weather conditions per season:
	+ Dry season (June to October): clear skies, mild temperatures
	+ Wet season (November to May): rain, mist, and slippery trails
* Local festivals/events worth attending:
	+ The Mount Kenya Marathon (June)
	+ The Kenya Wildlife Festival (September)

## 🥾 Adventure Activities & Itinerary
Here is a sample 7-day itinerary for trekking Mount Kenya:

### Day 1 — Arrival in Nairobi
* Arrive at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport
* Transfer to a hotel in Nairobi for overnight stay

### Day 2 — Nairobi to Nanyuki
* Take a bus or taxi from Nairobi to Nanyuki (3-4 hours)
* Check-in to a hotel in Nanyuki for overnight stay

### Day 3 — Nanyuki to Sirimon Gate
* Take a taxi or walk from Nanyuki to the Sirimon Gate (1-2 hours)
* Begin trekking to the Old Moses Camp (3-4 hours)

### Day 4 — Old Moses Camp to Shipton Camp
* Trek from Old Moses Camp to Shipton Camp (5-6 hours)
* Enjoy the scenic views of the surrounding landscape

### Day 5 — Acclimatization Day
* Spend the day acclimatizing to the high altitude
* Take a short trek to the nearby lakes and valleys

### Day 6 — Shipton Camp to Point Lenana
* Trek from Shipton Camp to Point Lenana (4-5 hours)
* Enjoy the sunrise from the summit

### Day 7 — Descent to Sirimon Gate
* Trek from Point Lenana to the Sirimon Gate (5-6 hours)
* Return to Nanyuki for overnight stay

Types of activities to cover:

* Trekking/Hiking: Sirimon Route, Naro Moru Route, Burguret Route
* Climbing: Point Lenana, Batian Peak, Nelion Peak
* Cultural visits: local villages, markets, and communities

## 🏕️ Accommodation & Basecamps
There are various accommodation options available for trekkers, ranging from budget-friendly camping sites to luxury lodges.

* Budget option: camping at the Old Moses Camp or Shipton Camp ($10 - $20 per night)
* Mid-range: guesthouses and lodges in Nanyuki ($50 - $100 per night)
* Premium: luxury lodges such as the Mount Kenya Safari Club ($200 - $500 per night)
* Wild camping: allowed in designated areas, but a permit is required

## 📋 Visa, Permits & Regulations
Here is a list of visa, permits, and regulations to consider:

* Visa requirements: a visa is required for most nationalities, and can be obtained online or at the airport
* Park Entry Permits: a permit is required to enter the Mount Kenya National Park, and can be obtained at the park gate
* Guide fees: a guide is mandatory for trekking Mount Kenya, and can be hired through a tour operator or at the park gate
* Environmental regulations:
	+ No littering or pollution
	+ No camping outside designated areas
	+ No hunting or fishing
* Nearest Embassy/Consulate contacts:
