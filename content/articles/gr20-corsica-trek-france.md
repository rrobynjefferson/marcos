## 🌄 Why Gr20 Corsica Trek France Complete Travel Guide?
The Gr20 Corsica Trek in France is a unique and must-visit destination for adventure travelers. This challenging trek offers breathtaking landscapes, diverse wildlife, and a rich cultural heritage. The trek spans over 180 kilometers, taking you through rugged mountains, scenic valleys, and picturesque villages. The highlights of this trek include the stunning views of the Mediterranean Sea, the majestic granite peaks, and the beautiful forests of Corsica. The best reasons to visit the Gr20 Corsica Trek include the spiritual experience of connecting with nature, the physical challenge of completing the trek, and the opportunity to immerse oneself in the local culture.

## ✈️ Cara Menuju ke Gr20 Corsica Trek France Complete Travel Guide
To get to the Gr20 Corsica Trek, you can fly from Indonesia to France and then take a domestic flight or ferry to Corsica.

### Dari Indonesia (via Jalur Udara)
The best international airport to fly from in Indonesia is Soekarno-Hatta International Airport (CGK) in Jakarta or Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS) in Bali. You can fly with airlines such as Garuda Indonesia, Singapore Airlines, or Emirates with a transit hub in Singapore, Dubai, or Paris.

* Estimated flight cost from Jakarta to Paris: IDR 15,000,000 - IDR 30,000,000 (USD 1,000 - USD 2,000)
* Estimated flight cost from Bali to Paris: IDR 18,000,000 - IDR 35,000,000 (USD 1,200 - USD 2,300)
* Recommended booking app/website: Skyscanner, Expedia, or Booking.com

### Dari Gateway City ke Lokasi (Last Mile)
The closest international airport to the Gr20 Corsica Trek is Ajaccio Napoleon Bonaparte Airport (AJA) or Bastia-Poretta Airport (BIA). From the airport, you can take a bus or taxi to the starting point of the trek.


> **✈️ Resource Perjalanan:** [Booking.com — Akomodasi Dunia](https://www.booking.com/)



* Bus: You can take a bus from Ajaccio to Calenzana, the starting point of the trek, with companies such as Corsica Bus or Autocars Corsica. The journey takes around 2-3 hours and costs around EUR 10-20 (IDR 150,000 - IDR 300,000).
* Taxi: You can take a taxi from the airport to Calenzana, which costs around EUR 100-150 (IDR 1,500,000 - IDR 2,200,000).

### Opsi Overland/Backpacker Route
If you prefer to take the overland route, you can take a ferry from France to Corsica. The ferry ride takes around 4-6 hours and costs around EUR 50-100 (IDR 750,000 - IDR 1,500,000).

* Ferry: You can take a ferry from Marseille or Nice to Ajaccio or Bastia with companies such as Corsica Ferries or SNCM. The journey takes around 4-6 hours and costs around EUR 50-100 (IDR 750,000 - IDR 1,500,000).

## 🗓️ Waktu Terbaik Berkunjung
The best time to visit the Gr20 Corsica Trek is from June to September, when the weather is warm and sunny. The peak season is in July and August, when the trek is busiest. The off-season is from October to May, when the weather is cooler and the trek is less crowded.

* Peak season: July and August
* Off-season: October to May
* Best time for hiking: June to September
* Warning: Avoid visiting during the winter months (December to February) when the trek is closed due to snow and harsh weather conditions.

## 📋 Visa, Permit & Regulasi
As an Indonesian citizen, you will need a visa to enter France. You can apply for a Schengen visa at the French embassy or consulate in Indonesia.

* Visa requirements: Valid passport, completed application form, recent passport-sized photo, proof of payment for visa fee
* Permit: No permit is required for the Gr20 Corsica Trek, but you will need to register at the refuge or park office before starting the trek.
* Regulasi: Be sure to follow the rules and regulations of the park, including no camping or campfires in sensitive areas, and no littering or pollution.

## ⚠️ Keselamatan & Risiko
The Gr20 Corsica Trek is a challenging trek with several risks, including:

* Altitude sickness
* Inclement weather
* Wild animals (sheep, goats, wild boar)
* Steep and rocky terrain
* Emergency contacts: Refuge de l'Onda, Refuge de la Croix, or the park office
* Travel insurance: Recommended to cover emergency medical evacuation and other unexpected expenses.

## 🌱 Etika & Responsible Travel
Here are some tips for responsible travel on the Gr20 Corsica Trek:

* Leave No Trace: Be sure to leave the natural environment as you found it, without litter or pollution.
* Respect local culture: Be respectful of the local culture and traditions, and try to learn a few words of the Corsican language.
* Support local economy: Be sure to support the local economy by buying food and supplies from local vendors and staying in local accommodations.
* Musim ini: Be sure to check the weather forecast before starting the trek, and be prepared for changing weather conditions.
