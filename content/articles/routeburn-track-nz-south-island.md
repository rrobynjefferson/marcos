## 🌄 Why Routeburn Track Nz South Island Complete Travel Guide?
The Routeburn Track is a 32km, 2-4 day trek located in the South Island of New Zealand, and it's a must-visit destination for any adventure traveler. This track offers a unique and unforgettable experience, with stunning mountain views, beautiful valleys, and picturesque lakes. The track takes you through two of New Zealand's most beautiful national parks: Fiordland National Park and Mount Aspiring National Park.

Some of the key attractions of the Routeburn Track include:
* The stunning views of the Darran Mountains and the Hollyford Valley
* The beautiful Lake Mackenzie and Lake Howden
* The picturesque Earland Falls and the Routeburn Falls
* The diverse and unique flora and fauna of the area

The Routeburn Track is a moderate-level trek, with some steep inclines and declines, but the scenery and the sense of accomplishment make it well worth the effort. The track is well-marked and well-maintained, making it accessible to hikers of all levels.

The best reasons to visit the Routeburn Track include:
* The spiritual experience of being in nature and surrounded by stunning scenery
* The physical challenge of completing the trek and pushing yourself to new limits
* The opportunity to experience the unique culture and history of the area
* The chance to see and experience the diverse and beautiful flora and fauna of the area

## 🗓️ Best Time to Visit
The best time to visit the Routeburn Track is from November to April, when the weather is warm and dry. The peak season is from December to February, when the track is busiest.

The weather conditions per season are:
* Summer (December to February): warm and dry, with average temperatures ranging from 15-25°C (59-77°F)
* Autumn (March to May): mild and cool, with average temperatures ranging from 10-20°C (50-68°F)

* Take a shuttle or bus from Queenstown to the track (around 1-2 hours)
* Start the trek at the Routeburn Track car park
* Hike to the Routeburn Flats Hut (around 2-3 hours)

* Guide requirements: a guide is not required, but it's recommended to hire a guide or join a guided tour if you're not experienced with hiking or trekking.

## ⚠️ Safety & Risks
Some major risks in the area include:
* Altitude sickness: the track reaches elevations of over 1,000m, and visitors should be aware of the risks of altitude sickness.
* Weather: the weather in the area can be unpredictable, and visitors should be prepared for rain, wind, and cold temperatures.
* Wildlife: the area is home to a variety of wildlife, including deer, pigs, and birds, and visitors should be aware of their surroundings and keep a safe distance.
* Emergency contacts: in case of an emergency, visitors can contact the local authorities or the DoC office in Queenstown.
