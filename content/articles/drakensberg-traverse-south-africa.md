## 🌄 Why Drakensberg Traverse South Africa Complete Travel Guide?
The Drakensberg Mountains, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, offer a unique and breathtaking experience for adventure travelers. Located in the eastern part of South Africa, the Drakensberg range stretches for over 1,000 kilometers, providing endless opportunities for hiking, trekking, and exploring. The Drakensberg Traverse is a challenging yet rewarding hike that takes you through some of the most spectacular landscapes in Africa.

* **Unique experiences:** The Drakensberg Traverse offers a chance to hike through diverse landscapes, from lush forests to rugged mountains, and experience the rich cultural heritage of the region.
* **Visual and sensory highlights:** The trail takes you through valleys, gorges, and mountain passes, offering breathtaking views of the surrounding landscape. You'll also have the opportunity to see a wide range of wildlife, including eland, baboons, and eagles.
* **Best reasons to visit:** The Drakensberg Traverse is a must-visit destination for outdoor enthusiasts, nature lovers, and adventure seekers. The trail offers a challenging physical experience, as well as a chance to connect with nature and local communities.

## ✈️ How to Get to Drakensberg Traverse South Africa Complete Travel Guide
Getting to the Drakensberg Mountains can be a bit challenging, but with the right information, you'll be well on your way.

### International Arrivals (Main Hubs)
The closest international airport to the Drakensberg Mountains is the **King Shaka International Airport (DUR)** in Durban, South Africa. You can fly into Durban from major hubs like **Johannesburg (JNB)**, **Cape Town (CPT)**, or **London (LHR)** with airlines like **South African Airways**, **British Airways**, or **Emirates**.

* **Estimated flight ticket price range:** $800 - $1,500 (low season), $1,200 - $2,500 (high season)
* **Best booking apps/websites:** **Expedia**, **Booking.com**, or **Skyscanner**

### From Gateway City to Location (Last Mile)
From Durban, you can take a domestic flight or drive to the nearest town, **Underberg**, which is the gateway to the Drakensberg Mountains.

* **Ground transport:** You can rent a car from **Avis** or **Europcar** at the Durban airport and drive to Underberg (approximately 2.5 hours). Alternatively, you can take a bus or shuttle service like **Intercape** or **Baz Bus** from Durban to Underberg.
* **REALISTIC travel duration:** 2.5 hours (driving), 3.5 hours (bus/shuttle)
* **Important transit points and checkpoints:** **Howick**, **Nottingham Road**, and **Himeville** are important transit points on the way to Underberg.

### Overland/Backpacker Route Options
If you're traveling on a budget or prefer to take the scenic route, you can consider taking a bus or hitchhiking from **Johannesburg** or **Cape Town** to Underberg.

* **Alternative land routes:** You can take the **N3 highway** from Johannesburg or the **N1 highway** from Cape Town to Durban, and then connect to Underberg via the **R617 road**.
* **Border crossings:** If you're coming from neighboring countries like **Lesotho** or **Mozambique**, you'll need to cross the border at **Laverton** or **Komatipoort**, respectively.

## 🗓️ Best Time to Visit
The best time to visit the Drakensberg Mountains is during the **spring (September to November)** and **autumn (March to May)** seasons, when the weather is mild and pleasant.

* **Peak season:** December to January (summer)
* **Off-season:** June to August (winter)
* **Weather conditions per season:**
	+ Spring: mild temperatures (15-25°C), occasional rain showers
	+ Summer: warm temperatures (20-30°C), frequent rain showers
	+ Autumn: mild temperatures (10-20°C), occasional rain showers
	+ Winter: cool temperatures (5-15°C), occasional snowfall
* **Local festivals/events worth attending:** The **Drakensberg Boys' Choir** performs regularly during the summer months, and the **Underberg Farmers' Market** takes place every Saturday.

## 🏕️ Accommodation & Basecamps
There are several accommodation options available in the Drakensberg Mountains, ranging from budget-friendly camping and hostels to luxury lodges and resorts.

* **Budget option:** Camping at **Injisuthi Campsite** ($10-20 per night) or staying at **Underberg Backpackers** ($20-30 per night)
* **Mid-range:** Staying at **The Old Hatchery** ($50-100 per night) or **Drakensberg Gardens Golf & Spa Resort** ($100-200 per night)
* **Premium:** Staying at **The Cavern** ($200-500 per night) or **Montusi Mountain Lodge** ($500-1,000 per night)
* **Wild camping:** Permitted in designated areas, but a permit is required ($10-20 per night)

## 📋 Visa, Permits & Regulations
When traveling to South Africa, it's essential to have the right visa and permits.

* **Visa requirements:** Citizens of most countries can enter South Africa without a visa, but it's essential to check with the **South African Department of Home Affairs** for specific requirements.
* **Park Entry Permits:** A permit is required to enter the **uKhahlamba-Drakensberg Park**, which can be obtained at the **park's entrance** ($10-20 per person)
* **Is a local guide mandatory?:** No, but it's highly recommended to hire a local guide to ensure a safe and successful journey.
* **Environmental regulations:** The **uKhahlamba-Drakensberg Park** has strict environmental regulations, including no littering, no camping outside designated areas, and no starting of fires.

## ⚠️ Safety & Risks
The Drakensberg Mountains can be a challenging and unpredictable environment, with risks including altitude sickness, inclement weather, and wildlife encounters.

* **Major risks:** Altitude sickness, inclement weather, wildlife encounters
* **Emergency contacts:** **South African National Parks** (SANParks) emergency number: +27 82 908 2122
* **Recommended Travel Insurance:** **World Nomads** or **Allianz Travel Insurance**
* **Common mistakes to avoid:** Underestimating the difficulty of the hike, not bringing enough water and food, not checking the weather forecast
* **Solo travel:** It's not recommended to travel alone in the Drakensberg Mountains, especially for inexperienced hikers.

## 🌱 Ethics & Responsible Travel
When traveling to the Drakensberg Mountains, it's essential to respect the local environment and communities.
