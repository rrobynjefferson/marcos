## ⚠️ Safety & Risks
The Laugavegur Trail can be a challenging and remote hike, and there are some safety risks you should be aware of:
* Altitude sickness: The trail reaches elevations of over 1,000m, and you may be at risk of altitude sickness.
* Weather: The weather on the trail can be unpredictable and severe, with rain, wind, and snow.
* River crossings: There are several river crossings on the trail, and you may need to use a bridge or ford the river.
* Wildlife: There are some wild animals on the trail, including sheep, horses, and birds.

Some of the specific safety precautions you should take on the Laugavegur Trail include:
* Acclimatizing to the altitude by spending a day or two in Reykjavik before starting the hike
* Checking the weather forecast before starting the hike and being prepared for changing weather conditions
* Using a guide or joining a guided tour if you are not experienced with river crossings or glacier travel
* Bringing a first aid kit and knowing how to use the items in it
* Bringing a map, compass, and GPS device or smartphone with GPS app, and knowing how to use them
