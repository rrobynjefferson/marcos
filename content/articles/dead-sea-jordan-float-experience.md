## 🗓️ Best Time to Visit
The best time to visit the Dead Sea in Jordan is from March to May or from September to November. The weather during these periods is mild and pleasant, with average temperatures ranging from 20-25°C (68-77°F).

* **Peak Season (June to August)**: The summer months are the peak tourist season, with high temperatures and humidity.
* **Off-Season (December to February)**: The winter months are the off-season, with cooler temperatures and fewer tourists.

Visitors should avoid visiting the Dead Sea during the summer months, as the heat and humidity can be extreme.

## ⚠️ Safety & Risks
The Dead Sea region is generally a safe destination, but visitors should be aware of the following risks:

* **Heat and dehydration**: The summer months can be extremely hot, and visitors should drink plenty of water and take regular breaks.
* **Swimming**: Visitors should be careful when swimming in the Dead Sea, as the water can be deep and the currents can be strong.
* **Hiking**: Visitors should be careful when hiking in the Dead Sea region, as the trails can be steep and rocky.
