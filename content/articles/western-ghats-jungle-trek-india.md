## 🌄 Why Western Ghats Jungle Trek India Complete Travel Guide?
The Western Ghats mountain range in India is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, known for its rich biodiversity and stunning natural beauty. This trek is a must-visit for adventure seekers and nature lovers, offering a unique blend of culture, history, and wildlife. The trek takes you through lush forests, rolling hills, and picturesque villages, providing breathtaking views of the surrounding landscape.

Some of the key attractions of the Western Ghats Jungle Trek include:

* The opportunity to spot exotic wildlife, such as elephants, tigers, and leopards
* The chance to explore ancient temples and ruins, such as the Hoysala temples and the Jain temples of Shravanabelagola
* The experience of trekking through diverse landscapes, including tropical rainforests, deciduous forests, and grasslands
* The opportunity to interact with local communities and learn about their culture and traditions
* The stunning views of the surrounding landscape, including the sunrise and sunset over the hills

The best reasons to visit the Western Ghats Jungle Trek include:

* Spiritual growth and self-discovery
* Physical challenge and adventure
* Nature and wildlife conservation
* Cultural immersion and exchange
* Personal growth and development

## ✈️ How to Get to Western Ghats Jungle Trek India Complete Travel Guide

### International Arrivals (Main Hubs)
The nearest international airport to the Western Ghats is the Bengaluru International Airport (BLR), which is located about 270 km from the trek starting point. Other nearby international airports include:

* Coimbatore International Airport (CJB)
* Kochi International Airport (COK)
* Thiruvananthapuram International Airport (TRV)

Recommended airlines and transit hubs include:

* Indian Airlines
* SpiceJet
* Air India
* Emirates
* Qatar Airways
* Singapore Airlines

Estimated flight ticket prices range from $500 to $1,500, depending on the season and departure city. The best booking apps and websites include:

* Expedia
* Booking.com
* Skyscanner
* Kayak
* MakeMyTrip

### From Gateway City to Location (Last Mile)
From Bengaluru, you can take a bus or taxi to the trek starting point. The nearest town to the trek starting point is Sakleshpur, which is about 220 km from Bengaluru.

* Bus: You can take a bus from Bengaluru to Sakleshpur, which takes about 4-5 hours. The bus fare is around $5-10. Bus companies like KSRTC and private operators like VRL and SRS run regular services.
* Taxi: You can hire a taxi from Bengaluru to Sakleshpur, which takes about 3-4 hours. The taxi fare is around $50-100.
* Train: You can also take a train from Bengaluru to Sakleshpur, which takes about 4-5 hours. The train fare is around $10-20.

Important transit points and checkpoints include:

* Bengaluru city
* Sakleshpur town
* Hassan district
* Chikmagalur district

### Overland/Backpacker Route Options
For budget travelers, there are several overland route options available. You can take a bus or train from nearby cities like Mysuru, Mangaluru, or Kochi to Sakleshpur.

* Bus: You can take a bus from Mysuru to Sakleshpur, which takes about 2-3 hours. The bus fare is around $5-10.
* Train: You can take a train from Mysuru to Sakleshpur, which takes about 2-3 hours. The train fare is around $10-20.

Border crossings are not required for this trek, as it is located within the state of Karnataka, India.

## 🗓️ Best Time to Visit
The best time to visit the Western Ghats Jungle Trek is from October to February, when the weather is cool and pleasant. The peak season is from December to January, when the trek is busiest.

* Peak season: December to January
* Off-season: March to May and September to November
* Weather conditions:
	+ Summer: March to May, hot and humid
	+ Monsoon: June to August, heavy rainfall
	+ Winter: September to February, cool and pleasant

Local festivals and events worth attending include:

* The Mysuru Dasara festival, which takes place in October
* The Sakleshpur festival, which takes place in November
* The Chikmagalur festival, which takes place in December

WARNING: The monsoon season, from June to August, is not recommended for trekking, as the trails can be slippery and dangerous.
