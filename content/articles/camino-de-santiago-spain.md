* Peak season: June to September, when the weather is warm and sunny, and the days are long.

## ⚠️ Safety & Risks
The Camino De Santiago is a relatively safe hike, but there are some risks and hazards to be aware of, including:

* Altitude sickness: The highest point on the trail is over 1,500 meters (4,921 feet), and pilgrims should be aware of the risks of altitude sickness.
* Wildlife: The trail passes through areas with wildlife, including bears, wolves, and snakes.
* Weather: The weather on the trail can be unpredictable, with rain, snow, and heat waves possible.
* Crime: As with any tourist destination, there is a risk of crime, including pickpocketing and theft.
