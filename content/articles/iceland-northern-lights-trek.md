## 🗓️ Best Time to Visit
The best time to visit Iceland for the Northern Lights Trek is between September and April, when the nights are dark and the skies are clear. The peak season for the trek is between December and March, when the Northern Lights are at their most active.

The weather in Iceland can be unpredictable, with sudden changes in temperature and precipitation. It is essential to be prepared for all types of weather, including rain, snow, and wind.

Some of the local festivals and events worth attending include:
* The Reykjavik International Film Festival, which takes place in September
* The Iceland Airwaves music festival, which takes place in November
* The Christmas markets in Reykjavik, which take place in December

The bad seasons to avoid are:
* The summer months (June to August), when the days are long and the nights are light, making it difficult to see the Northern Lights
* The winter months (December to February), when the weather can be harsh and the roads may be closed due to snow and ice
