## 🗓️ Best Time to Visit
The best time to visit the Sahara Desert in Morocco is during the winter months (October to February) when the temperatures are mild and the sun is not too strong. The peak season is from December to March, and the off-season is from April to June and September to November.

Weather conditions per season include:

* Winter (October to February): mild temperatures, 15-20°C (59-68°F)
* Spring (March to May): warm temperatures, 20-25°C (68-77°F)
* Summer (June to August): hot temperatures, 30-40°C (86-104°F)
* Autumn (September to November): warm temperatures, 20-25°C (68-77°F)

Local festivals and events worth attending include:

* The Marrakech International Film Festival in December
* The Fez Festival of World Sacred Music in June
* The Erfoud Date Festival in October

Warning: bad seasons to avoid include:

* The summer months (June to August) due to the extreme heat
* The winter months (December to February) if you are not prepared for the cold nights

## 🥾 Adventure Activities & Itinerary
Here is a realistic day-by-day itinerary for a 7-day Sahara Desert trekking trip:

### Day 1 — Arrival in Marrakech
* Arrive at Marrakech Menara Airport (RAK)
* Transfer to your hotel in the old medina
* Explore the city, including the Jemaa el-Fnaa square and the Koutoubia Mosque

### Day 2 — Marrakech to Merzouga
* Take the bus or taxi from Marrakech to Merzouga (Erg Chebbi)
* Check-in to your hotel or riad
* Explore the town and the Erg Chebbi dunes

### Day 3 — Erg Chebbi Dunes
* Take a camel trek into the Erg Chebbi dunes
* Watch the sunrise over the dunes
* Return to Merzouga for lunch
* Explore the town and the local market

### Day 4 — Merzouga to Erg Chigaga
* Take the bus or taxi from Merzouga to M'Hamid (Erg Chigaga)
* Check-in to your hotel or riad
* Explore the town and the Erg Chigaga dunes

### Day 5 — Erg Chigaga Dunes
* Take a camel trek into the Erg Chigaga dunes
* Watch the sunset over the dunes
* Return to M'Hamid for dinner
* Enjoy the local music and dance

### Day 6 — Erg Chigaga to Ouarzazate
* Take the bus or taxi from M'Hamid to Ouarzazate
* Check-in to your hotel or riad

### Day 7 — Ouarzazate to Marrakech
* Take the bus or taxi from Ouarzazate to Marrakech
* Return to Marrakech Menara Airport (RAK) for your departure flight
