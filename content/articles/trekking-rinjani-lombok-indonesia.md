## 🌄 Why Trekking Rinjani Lombok Indonesia Complete Travel Guide?
Trekking Rinjani Lombok Indonesia is a unique and must-visit destination for adventure travelers. Located in the heart of Lombok, Indonesia, Mount Rinjani is an active volcano that offers breathtaking views, diverse wildlife, and a spiritual experience. The summit of Mount Rinjani is 3,726 meters above sea level, making it one of the highest peaks in Indonesia. The trek to the summit is challenging, but the scenery and sense of accomplishment make it an unforgettable experience.

The main attractions of Trekking Rinjani Lombok Indonesia include:
* **Breathtaking views**: The summit of Mount Rinjani offers stunning views of the surrounding landscape, including the crater lake, Segara Anak, and the island of Lombok.
* **Diverse wildlife**: The Mount Rinjani National Park is home to a wide range of flora and fauna, including monkeys, birds, and wild boars.
* **Spiritual experience**: The trek to the summit of Mount Rinjani is a spiritual experience, with many locals believing that the mountain has healing properties.
* **Challenging trek**: The trek to the summit of Mount Rinjani is challenging, with steep inclines and uneven terrain, making it a great destination for adventure travelers.

## ✈️ How to Get to Trekking Rinjani Lombok Indonesia Complete Travel Guide

### International Arrivals (Main Hubs)
The nearest international airport to Lombok is **Lombok International Airport (LOP)**, which is located in the southern part of the island. The airport is served by several international airlines, including **Garuda Indonesia**, **Lion Air**, and **Malaysia Airlines**. The estimated flight ticket price range from major hubs is:
* **Low season (October to April)**: $500-$1,000 USD return from Singapore or Kuala Lumpur.
* **High season (May to September)**: $800-$1,500 USD return from Singapore or Kuala Lumpur.
The best booking apps/websites for flights to Lombok are **Expedia**, **Skyscanner**, and **Booking.com**.

### From Gateway City to Location (Last Mile)
From Lombok International Airport, the nearest local city with an airport is **Mataram**, which is the capital city of Lombok. The best way to get to Mataram from the airport is by **taxi** or **rental car**, which takes around 30-40 minutes and costs around $10-$20 USD.
From Mataram, the best way to get to the starting point of the trek, **Senaru Village**, is by **bus** or **rental car**, which takes around 2-3 hours and costs around $20-$50 USD. The bus company **Damri** operates daily services from Mataram to Senaru Village.
The important transit points and checkpoints are:
* **Mataram**: The capital city of Lombok, where you can find accommodation, food, and transportation.
* **Senaru Village**: The starting point of the trek, where you can find accommodation, food, and guides.
* **Rinjani National Park**: The national park where the trek takes place, where you need to register and pay the entrance fee.

### Overland/Backpacker Route Options
For budget travelers, the best overland route to Lombok is by **ferry** from **Bali**. The ferry ride takes around 4-5 hours and costs around $10-$20 USD. From the ferry terminal in Lombok, you can take a **bus** or **rental car** to Mataram, which takes around 1-2 hours and costs around $10-$20 USD.
The border crossing from Bali to Lombok is by **ferry**, which operates daily from **Padangbai** in Bali to **Lembar** in Lombok.

## 🗓️ Best Time to Visit
The best time to visit Lombok and trek Mount Rinjani is during the **dry season**, which is from **May to September**. The weather is dry and sunny, with average temperatures ranging from 20-30°C.
The peak season is from **June to August**, when the weather is dry and sunny, and the trekking conditions are ideal.
The off-season is from **October to April**, when the weather is wet and humid, and the trekking conditions are challenging.

## 🥾 Adventure Activities & Itinerary

* Distance: 60 km, 2-3 hours.
* Elevation: 600 meters.

### Day 2 — Trek to Crater Rim
* Start the trek from Senaru Village and ascend to the crater rim.
* The trek is challenging, with steep inclines and uneven terrain.
* Distance: 8 km, 6-7 hours.
* Elevation: 2,600 meters.

### Day 3 — Explore Crater Rim and Summit
* Explore the crater rim and enjoy the stunning views of the surrounding landscape.
* Ascend to the summit of Mount Rinjani, which is 3,726 meters above sea level.
* Distance: 10 km, 8-9 hours.
* Elevation: 3,726 meters.

### Day 4 — Descend to Lake and Hot Springs
* Descend from the summit to the crater lake, Segara Anak.
* Enjoy the stunning views of the lake and the surrounding landscape.
* Soak in the hot springs, which are believed to have healing properties.
* Distance: 10 km, 6-7 hours.
* Elevation: 2,000 meters.

### Day 5 — Return to Senaru Village
* Return to Senaru Village by **bus** or **rental car**.
* Check-out of the hotel or guesthouse and transfer to the airport for departure.
* Distance: 60 km, 2-3 hours.
* Elevation: 600 meters.
