The best way to reach the Georgia Caucasus Mountains is by flying into Tbilisi International Airport (TBS), which is located about 15 kilometers east of the city center. ### From Gateway City to Location (Last Mile)
From Tbilisi, you can take a bus or taxi to the nearby city of Stepantsminda, which is the starting point for most treks in the Georgia Caucasus Mountains. The journey takes about 3-4 hours by bus, depending on traffic and road conditions.

Ground transport options include:
* Bus: companies like Metrobus and Georgian Bus operate daily services from Tbilisi to Stepantsminda
* Taxi: private taxis are available from Tbilisi to Stepantsminda, but be sure to negotiate the price beforehand
* Jeep: 4WD vehicles can be rented in Tbilisi for the journey to Stepantsminda, but be sure to check the road conditions and weather forecast beforehand

Realistic travel duration and important transit points include:
* Tbilisi to Stepantsminda: 3-4 hours by bus, depending on traffic and road conditions
* Stepantsminda to Gergeti: 1-2 hours by taxi or jeep, depending on road conditions
* Gergeti to Juta: 2-3 hours by foot, depending on your trekking pace and the terrain

## 🗓️ Best Time to Visit
The best time to visit the Georgia Caucasus Mountains is from June to September, when the weather is warm and sunny, and the trekking trails are open. The peak season is July and August, when the weather is at its warmest, but also the busiest.

Weather conditions per season include:
* Spring (March to May): mild temperatures, with occasional rain and snow
* Summer (June to September): warm temperatures, with occasional thunderstorms
* Autumn (September to November): cool temperatures, with occasional rain and snow
* Winter (December to February): cold temperatures, with frequent snow and ice

Local festivals and events worth attending include:
* The Tbilisi International Festival, which takes place in July
* The Kazbegi Festival, which takes place in August
* The Gergeti Festival, which takes place in September

WARNING: the bad seasons to avoid are:
* Winter (December to February), when the trekking trails are closed due to heavy snow and ice
* Early spring (March to April), when the weather is unpredictable and the trails are still snow-covered
