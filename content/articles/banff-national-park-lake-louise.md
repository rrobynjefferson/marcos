## 🌄 Why Banff National Park Lake Louise Complete Travel Guide?
Banff National Park and Lake Louise are two of the most breathtaking destinations in the Canadian Rockies, offering a unique blend of natural beauty, outdoor adventure, and spiritual experiences. Located in the heart of the Rockies, Banff National Park is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, featuring towering mountain peaks, turquoise lakes, and abundant wildlife. Lake Louise, with its emerald green waters and surrounding mountains, is a picturesque village that offers a wide range of outdoor activities and stunning scenery.

Some of the key attractions and experiences that make Banff National Park and Lake Louise a must-visit destination include:
* Hiking and trekking through scenic trails, such as the Johnston Canyon Trail and the Lake Louise Lakeshore Trail
* Taking a scenic drive along the Icefields Parkway, one of the most beautiful highways in the world
* Exploring the stunning glaciers and icefields of the Columbia Icefield
* Enjoying water sports, such as kayaking or canoeing, on Lake Louise or Moraine Lake
* Visiting the charming town of Banff, with its many shops, restaurants, and cafes
* Spotting wildlife, such as grizzly bears, wolves, and elk, in their natural habitat
* Taking a scenic gondola ride to the top of Sulphur Mountain for panoramic views of the town and surrounding mountains

The visual and sensory highlights of Banff National Park and Lake Louise include:
* The vibrant turquoise color of Lake Louise and Moraine Lake
* The stunning mountain scenery, with peaks reaching over 3,000 meters
* The abundant wildlife, including grizzly bears, wolves, and elk
* The picturesque village of Lake Louise, with its many shops and restaurants
* The scenic drives, including the Icefields Parkway and the Bow Valley Parkway
* The many hiking and trekking trails, including the Johnston Canyon Trail and the Lake Louise Lakeshore Trail

## 🗓️ Best Time to Visit
The best time to visit Banff National Park and Lake Louise is from June to September, when the weather is warm and sunny, and the wildflowers are in bloom. The peak season is from July to August, when the park is busiest and the prices are highest.

The weather conditions per season are:
* Spring (April to May): Cool and wet, with some snow still on the ground
* Summer (June to August): Warm and sunny, with average highs of 22-25°C (72-77°F)
* Autumn (September to October): Cool and dry, with some snow on the ground
* Winter (November to March): Cold and snowy, with average lows of -10 to -20°C (14-4°F)

Some of the local festivals and events worth attending include:
* The Banff World Tour Film Festival (November)
* The Lake Louise Winter Festival (February)
* The Banff Summer Arts Festival (June to August)
* The Lake Louise Marathon (June)

## 🥾 Adventure Activities & Itinerary
Here is a realistic day-by-day itinerary for a 7-10 day trip to Banff National Park and Lake Louise:

### Day 1 — Arrival in Banff
* Arrive in Calgary International Airport and take a shuttle or bus to Banff
* Check into a hotel or hostel and explore the town of Banff
* Visit the Banff National Park Visitor Centre to get information on hiking trails and outdoor activities

### Day 2 — Hiking in Banff National Park
* Hike the Johnston Canyon Trail, a 5.5 kilometer (3.4 mile) trail that takes approximately 2-3 hours to complete
* Visit the Lower Falls and Upper Falls, two of the most scenic waterfalls in the park
* Return to Banff and enjoy dinner at one of the many restaurants in town

### Day 3 — Scenic Drive to Lake Louise
* Take a scenic drive along the Icefields Parkway to Lake Louise, approximately 57 kilometers (35 miles) west of Banff
* Visit the Lake Louise Lakeshore Trail, a 6 kilometer (3.7 mile) trail that takes approximately 2-3 hours to complete
* Enjoy dinner at one of the many restaurants in Lake Louise

### Day 4 — Kayaking on Lake Louise
* Rent a kayak and paddle on Lake Louise, approximately $20-$30 CAD per hour
* Visit the Lake Louise Canoe Dock, a scenic spot with stunning views of the surrounding mountains
* Return to Lake Louise and enjoy dinner at one of the many restaurants in town

### Day 5 — Hiking in Lake Louise
* Hike the Plain of Six Glaciers Trail, a 5.5 kilometer (3.4 mile) trail that takes approximately 2-3 hours to complete
* Visit the Plain of Six Glaciers Teahouse, a scenic spot with stunning views of the surrounding mountains
* Return to Lake Louise and enjoy dinner at one of the many restaurants in town

### Day 6 — Scenic Drive to Moraine Lake
* Take a scenic drive to Moraine Lake, approximately 14 kilometers (9 miles) south of Lake Louise
* Visit the Moraine Lake Shoreline Trail, a 3 kilometer (1.9 mile) trail that takes approximately 1-2 hours to complete
* Enjoy dinner at one of the many restaurants in Lake Louise

### Day 7 — Return to Banff
* Return to Banff and visit the Banff Gondola, a scenic spot with stunning views of the surrounding mountains
* Enjoy dinner at one of the many restaurants in Banff
