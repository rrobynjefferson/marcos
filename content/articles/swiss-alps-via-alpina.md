## 🗓️ Best Time to Visit
The best time to visit the Swiss Alps Via Alpina is from June to September, when the weather is warm and sunny, and the trails are open. The peak season is July and August, when the weather is usually warm and sunny, but it can also be crowded.

Weather conditions per season include:
* Spring (April to May): The weather is mild and sunny, with some snow still on the higher peaks.
* Summer (June to September): The weather is warm and sunny, with occasional thunderstorms.
* Autumn (October to November): The weather is cool and sunny, with some snow on the higher peaks.
* Winter (December to March): The weather is cold and snowy, with many trails closed due to avalanche risk.

Local festivals and events worth attending include:
* Unspunnenfest: A traditional Swiss festival held every three years in Interlaken, featuring traditional music, dance, and food.
* Grindelwald World Snow Festival: A winter festival held in Grindelwald, featuring snow sculptures, live music, and skiing competitions.
* Jungfraujoch Ice Magic: A winter festival held at the Jungfraujoch, featuring ice sculptures, live music, and skiing competitions.

WARNING: Bad seasons to avoid include:
* Winter (December to March): Many trails are closed due to avalanche risk, and the weather can be cold and snowy.
* Early spring (April to May): Some trails may still be closed due to snow, and the weather can be unpredictable.
